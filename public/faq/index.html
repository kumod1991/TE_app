What is TradeEdge?

TradeEdge is an Indian stock market analytics workspace for screening stocks, reviewing company data, tracking market breadth, following institutional flows, and journaling trades.

Is TradeEdge a trading app or a research tool?

It is primarily a research and journaling tool. It helps users organize information and review decisions, but it does not replace independent judgment.

Who should use TradeEdge?

Traders and investors who follow Indian equities, care about process, and want a cleaner workflow for screening and review usually get the most value.

Why does the site have so many pages?

The site groups different research tasks into focused pages so users can move from broad market context to specific work without rebuilding the same search every time.

Does TradeEdge provide investment advice?

No. It is a technology and analytics platform. Users are responsible for their own decisions and should review the legal and disclaimer pages before relying on the outputs.
