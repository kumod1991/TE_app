Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

TradeEdge ("we," "us," or "our") operates the financial analytics and trade tracking workspace at tradeedge.in. This Privacy Policy details how we handle user information, data security, and third-party advertising cookies.

1. Information We Collect

We collect limited user information to provide authentication, save watchlists, and maintain trade journal records:

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication credentials handled securely via Supabase / Google OAuth.
  • User Data: Custom watchlists, stock screeners, portfolio holdings, and trade journal entries configured by you.
  • Technical & Analytics Data: IP address, device specs, browser type, and aggregate platform usage logs.

2. Third-Party Advertising and Google AdSense Cookies

TradeEdge uses Google AdSense and third-party advertising partners to display relevant advertisements on our site.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to TradeEdge or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or AboutAds.info.

3. Data Protection and Security

We enforce row-level security controls, HTTPS encryption in transit, and secure cloud authentication. We do not sell or rent personal account details to data brokers or third parties.

4. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to inspect, export, or permanently delete your account data at any time. For privacy requests or account removal, contact our privacy desk at kmk03072018@gmail.com.
